# Hangry Worms

A pixel-art worm arena for phones and computers. Eat junk food to grow, shoot other worms to knock the food out of them, and fart on your friends.

Play: https://1236benjamin-blip.github.io/hangry-worms/

- Solo against bots, 2 players on one screen, or online rooms for up to 4 (host shares a 4-letter code).
- Touch (phone held sideways), keyboard + mouse, and game controllers.
- Plain static site: no build step, no server. Open `index.html` or serve the folder.

See [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) for how it is built, design decisions and known issues.
